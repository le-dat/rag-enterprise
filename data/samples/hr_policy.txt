Company HR Policy Guidelines (v1.2)

1. Working Hours
The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday. Core collaboration hours are from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Employees are expected to be available online or in-office during these core hours.

2. Annual Leave entitlement
All employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave. Any leave request must be submitted via the HR portal at least two weeks before the planned departure. Managers are responsible for approving or declining requests within 3 business days.

3. Probation Period
New employees are subject to a 3-month probation period. During this time, either party can terminate the employment agreement with 1-week written notice.

4. Performance Reviews
We conduct performance reviews twice a year: a mid-year checkin in July and a full year-end evaluation in December. Promotions and adjustments are decided during the December cycle.
